About the Game

Hafte Kasif (The Dirty Seven) is a Turkish card game for 3-4 players. Players take turns playing cards, using special card effects to outmaneuver opponents. The goal is to empty your hand first — but watch out for reversals!

Special Cards

  • 7 — Next player draws 2 (or chains with another 7)
  • Ace — Play again! Chain 4 aces for a mega-bonus
  • Jack — Change the suit, worth 2 points when finishing
  • Queen — Reveal a random card from an opponent
  • King — Reverse direction of play
  • 10 — Skip the next player
  • 2 — Give a card to the next player
