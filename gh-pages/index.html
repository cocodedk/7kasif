What is Hafte Kasif?

🎴

Hafte Kasif (also known as Bisheori) is a card game variant combining elements of Uno and other trick-taking games. The rules are... let's say locally defined and fiercely debated.

If you're not part of our group, you probably won't understand the scoring. And that's okay.
