About the Game

Hafte Kasif (The Dirty Seven) is a card game variant combining elements of Uno and other trick-taking games — what happens when Uno has a chaotic Danish-Persian cousin. The rules are... let's say locally defined and fiercely debated.

If you're not part of our group, you probably won't understand the scoring. And that's okay.
